Privacy Policy and Acceptable Use

Last updated 2026-10-20

PicoPaaS is a development and test platform run by an individual. Access is by request, a platform admin decides which accounts to approve, and it is provided as is, best effort, with no SLA. This page explains in plain language what we collect and what we expect from users.

Data we collect

  • Account email address and a password hash (we never store your password).
  • App configuration you provide: image names, ports, env vars and secrets. Env var values are encrypted at rest.
  • Operational logs and audit entries (for example log-ins, approvals, suspensions, deployments).
  • Contents of databases and buckets you create, which you control.

Why we collect it

To run your account and apps, keep the platform secure, investigate abuse, and debug problems. Nothing else.

Retention and deletion

Data may be deleted at any time, without notice. Account and app data is kept while your account exists, as best effort. Deleting an app removes its database, bucket and configuration. You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time, or an administrator may delete it. Logs and audit entries are kept for a limited period for security purposes.

No selling of data

We do not sell or share your data for advertising or marketing.

Sub-processors

  • DigitalOcean: hosting, managed database and object storage.
  • Let's Encrypt: TLS certificates (hostnames of your apps appear in public certificate transparency logs).

Cookies

We set a single session cookie to keep you logged in. No analytics or advertising cookies.

Acceptable use

You must not use PicoPaaS for:

  • illegal content or activity;
  • malware, phishing or other deceptive sites;
  • spam or SMTP/email abuse;
  • cryptocurrency mining;
  • attacking, scanning or interfering with other tenants or the platform itself.

Enforcement

The operator may suspend or delete any account or app, with or without notice, for any violation of these rules or if the platform is at risk.

No warranty

The service is provided as is and best effort, without warranty of any kind and with no SLA or uptime guarantee. Do not store personal or sensitive data here. It is for throwaway projects: it may be slow, change, or disappear, and data may be lost. Keep your own backups.

Contact

Abuse and privacy reports, and deletion requests: abuse@picopaas.xyz
